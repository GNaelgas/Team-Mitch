Refund policy: what to tell customers

No refunds on active accounts. Overpayment stays as a credit applied to future invoices.
Overpayment / creditCredit remains on the account and is automatically applied to the next billing cycle before charging the card.
Example$55 credit on a $25/mo account covers the next 2 invoices; the card is only charged again after the credit is used up.
RefundsOnly considered when the account is cancelled, with no pending billings and no outstanding invoices.
Thiranga Perera, Director of Finance

Cards on file: billing update

New cards are now added to the system automatically.

Check off all invoices (recurring, service, etc.) so we can charge for everything on file, not just the recurring.
Outstanding Balance on file are now authorized to deduct on turned on Automatic ("Y") Preauthorized Payment Method on File. Do not charge on turned off ("N") PAP, unless specified by customer
