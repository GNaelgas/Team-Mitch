Suspension Process

Set expectation: 72-business hour turnaround for a response.

Customer sends email request

  • Forward the request
    To customerservice@apialarm.com — French customers go to servicequebec@apialarm.com
  • Set the subject line
    Suspension Request // Sedona # // Stages/SIMS # // Group Code
    If details unknown — confirm with customer first
  • Write the email body
    Greeting to internal team + customer requesting suspension of services
  • Log it
    Copy/paste the email into Sedona notes and add a note that the customer called in to suspend

Customer calls requesting suspension

  • Direct them to email
    customerservice@apialarm.com — French customers to servicequebec@apialarm.com
  • Provide the account number
    Give the customer their Sedona account number to include in their request
  • Log it
    Add notes in Sedona confirming the customer called in to suspend services
— Created by Rhonda Petrash, Sales Manager (Mar 18, 2026)

Refund Policy — What To Tell Customers

No refunds on active accounts. Overpayment stays as a credit applied to future invoices.
Overpayment / CreditCredit remains on account and is automatically applied to the next billing cycle before charging the card.
Example$55 credit on a $25/mo account → next 2 invoices covered; card only charged again after credit is used up.
RefundsOnly considered when the account is cancelled, no pending billings, and no outstanding invoices.
— Thiranga Perera, Director of Finance
